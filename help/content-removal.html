Content Removal

This page explains how to remove content from StreamHub, whether you're the account holder who uploaded it or someone who appears in it and did not consent to its posting.

I uploaded this content

Sign in and delete it directly from your account dashboard — no request needed. Removal is typically immediate. If you can't access your account, use the form below and select "Account Holder / Uploader."

I appear in this content

If content depicting you was posted without your consent, or you withdrew consent after it was posted, use the form below. These requests are reviewed on a priority basis.

1. Who Can Request Removal

  • The account holder who uploaded the content;
  • A person who appears in the content and did not consent, or has since withdrawn consent;
  • A parent or legal guardian acting on behalf of a minor;
  • A copyright owner — use our DMCA Notice & Takedown Policy instead, since copyright claims follow a separate legal process.

2. What Happens After You Submit a Request

  1. We confirm receipt of your request;
  2. Our moderation team reviews the content and your request, typically within seven (7) business days;
  3. If the request is valid, we remove or disable access to the content and notify the uploader, where applicable;
  4. If we need more information to process your request, we'll contact you at the email address you provide;
  5. If you disagree with our decision on a consent-based removal request, you may appeal; see Section 4 below.

3. Identity Verification

To protect against fraudulent removal requests, we may ask you to verify your identity or your connection to the content before we act on a request — for example, by confirming details only the depicted person or account holder would know. This does not apply to copyright (DMCA) requests, which follow the verification process described in our DMCA policy.

4. Appeals

If you disagree with a decision we made on a consent-based removal request, you may appeal. Disputes that cannot be resolved directly may be submitted to a neutral, third-party arbitration process at our expense. Contact us using the form below to start an appeal.

If this involves content you believe depicts a minor, please use our Report Content form and select "Content Involving a Minor" instead of the form below — those reports are routed for immediate review.
