Access to StreamHub is restricted to individuals who are 18 years of age or older (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, whichever is higher), as set out in our Terms of Service. We disclaim responsibility for any misrepresentation of age by a user. We encourage parents and guardians to use the tools below to help prevent minors from accessing this Site.

1. Restricted To Adults (RTA) Labeling

Pages on this Site include the industry-standard "Restricted to Adults" (RTA) label, which allows parental control software and filtering tools to automatically block access for devices configured with those protections.

2. Search Engine Safe Search

Most major search engines offer a "Safe Search" setting that filters explicit content from search results and can be applied to individual accounts, managed family accounts, or school/workplace networks. We recommend enabling this setting on any device used by a minor.

3. Operating System & Device Controls

Modern operating systems and devices include built-in parental control features that let you restrict access to adult websites, set screen-time limits, and manage app permissions, including on:

  • iOS and macOS devices (Screen Time / Family Sharing settings)
  • Android devices (Family Link and device-level content restrictions)
  • Windows (Family Safety settings)
  • Kids-oriented tablet and app ecosystems with built-in content filters

4. Dedicated Parental Control Software

Third-party parental control software can block adult websites at the network or device level, including sites that use RTA labeling. Several reputable options exist; search for "parental control software" along with your device type to find one that fits your household's needs.

5. General Digital Safety Resources

Numerous non-profit and government-affiliated organizations publish guidance for parents on internet safety, age-appropriate content, and having conversations with children about safe internet use. We encourage parents and guardians to seek out these resources as part of a broader approach to online safety, in addition to technical filtering tools.

6. Reporting Concerns

If you believe a minor has accessed this Site or that content involving a minor has appeared on this Site, please contact us immediately using our support/report contact so we can investigate.
